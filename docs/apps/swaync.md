# swaync

Notifications and a minimal top-right CAD log drawer (`swaync`), styled in the Viewport look: 0px opaque `vp_bar`, 1px `vp_cyan` 45% frame, 8px/32px cyan header ruler, Terminess pixel type, `vp_magenta` unread accent and `vp_crit` critical outline.

Toasts and the control center open 6px below the 38px top bar and 7px from the right edge. The drawer keeps only `NOTIFICATIONS` (with `CLEAR`), `DO NOT DISTURB` and the notification list — media playback and volume/brightness live on waybar and swayosd. The top bar's bell toggles the drawer on left click, clears all on middle click and toggles do-not-disturb on right click.
