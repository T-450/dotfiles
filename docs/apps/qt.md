# qt

Qt 6 apps (qutebrowser today): native Wayland, dark or light from the palette's variant.

`QT_QPA_PLATFORMTHEME=xdgdesktopportal` (in `mango/.config/mango/env.conf` and `environment.d`) makes Qt follow the settings portal's `color-scheme`, which `gtk/reload.sh` sets from the palette: the Fusion style in its dark or light palette, Qt's own grays rather than the palette's colors. Qt picks that theme by itself only inside flatpak, so the variable is needed. Apps started after `theme set` get the new variant; running ones keep theirs. Without it Qt is light on every palette. qutebrowser's pages and chrome take the palette's colors from its own `colors.py` ([qutebrowser](qutebrowser.md)).

Palette colors in Qt widgets would need qt6ct (its upstream is archived and it builds against Qt's private headers) or Kvantum (hex themes that ignore the portal); neither is worth it while qutebrowser is the only Qt app.
