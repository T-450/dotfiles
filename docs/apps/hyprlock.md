# hyprlock

The lock screen (super+esc l, the power menu, and after 5 min idle).

hyprlock replaced swaylock so the lock screen can follow the Quiet Instrument philosophy: your wallpaper (`~/.local/state/wallpaper/current`, what `wallpaper-set` points at) blurred and at half brightness behind one raised panel (`bg_widget` at 85%, the panel radius, no border or shadow) holding the time in light `fg_bright`, the date as a lowercase `fg_muted` label and a slim password field. The wallpaper, not a screenshot: nothing that was on screen shows through the lock. The field's edge is the `accent` (the one thing that matters on this screen), `error` with "wrong password" on a failed attempt and `warning` while Caps Lock is on. Fades last 150 ms on the desktop's one curve. With no wallpaper the backdrop is `bg_desktop`.

Every lock starts in the background (`pidof -q hyprlock || hyprlock &`): hyprlock runs until unlock and has no fork-once-locked flag, and `swayidle -w` waits for each command. Before suspend it skips the fade and swayidle waits a second so the lock is up first (see [Session](../session.md)).
