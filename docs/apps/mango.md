# mango

The compositor (mangowm 0.18): config, keybindings, window rules, the session environment and its helper commands.

`config.conf` sources `colors.conf` (rendered) and `env.conf bind.conf rule.conf monitor.conf tag.conf`; `bind.conf` defines `var=` shortcuts (`$term $launcher $locker $browser`) before use. Mango re-reads its config on save (also super+r). Tiled windows have no gaps (`gap_*=0` in `config.conf`): they meet edge to edge, like tmux or Neovim panes. There are no gap keybindings.

Borders are drawn like tmux's pane lines: 3px, square corners, a grey line (`border_inactive` mixed toward `fg_muted`) on every window and green (`success`) on the focused one, so two panes meet at a 6px seam that is half green next to the focused pane (mango has one border width for every window; only the color marks focus). Green is used by no other state, so focus never shares a color with maximized (`accent`), scratchpad (`badge` mixed toward `fg_bright`), urgent (`error`), global or overlay windows. A lone window has no border (`no_border_when_single=1`). Every window is fully opaque (`unfocused_opacity=1.0`): the border alone marks focus. The tab bar follows the same rule (3px, square, green focused tab). `mango-quit` is the logout (see [Session](../session.md)); `mango-monitor` toggles the laptop panel (super+p); `mango-keys` shows every binding as a rofi cheatsheet (super+/), read from `bind.conf`: `## ` lines are its sections and a `#: ` line describes the bind below it. Caps Lock is Escape.

Every animation lasts 150 ms (9 frames at 60 Hz) on one curve, a quadratic ease-out with no overshoot (`0.5,1,0.89,1`). Fades start at 0.5 (opening) and 0.6 (closing) opacity; zooms grow from 0.1 and shrink to 0.6 of full size. Blur is off, so every overlay is opaque. Shadows are on for floating windows only (`shadow_only_floating=1`). Layer shadows stay enabled but no overlay shows one: a layer shadow outlines the layer's whole surface, not what it draws, and rofi's surface spans the screen above the bar, so `rule.conf` turns its shadow off; notifications have `no_shadow` and the bar never gets one. `blur` and `shadows` are master switches: `blur_layer` and `layer_shadows` do nothing without them. On a busy test workload, 150 ms durations alone halved mango's CPU time (2.51 to 1.24 percent) and the GPU's busy time (11.4 to 6.5 percent), measured before shadows were turned on; the full change was not measured. Why these values: [design](../designs/mango-animations.md).
