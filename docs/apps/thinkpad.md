# thinkpad

ThinkPad T14 hardware: microcode, audio DSP firmware, Intel video drivers, fingerprint reader, firmware updates and TLP power management.

TLP alone manages power (`root/etc/tlp.d/10-thinkpad.conf`). Deliberately not installed: power-profiles-daemon (conflicts with TLP) and thermald (exits on this T14; the firmware handles thermals).

powertop is installed as a diagnostic only (per-process CPU use and wakeups); its `--auto-tune` and `powertop.service` are never used, because TLP alone manages power.

Thunderbolt: the BIOS security level is `user`, so a Thunderbolt device's PCIe tunnel stays closed until it is authorized. `bolt` does that: `boltctl list` shows devices, `boltctl enroll <uuid>` authorizes one for good (USB and DisplayPort over the port work without it). `setup.sh` turns VT-d and Kernel DMA Protection on in the BIOS through thinklmi (`/sys/class/firmware-attributes/thinklmi`, no BIOS admin password set), so the kernel turns the IOMMU on and a device on that port can't read memory directly; a change shows as `bios    NAME=VALUE` and takes effect at the next boot. Check after it with `fwupdmgr security` (IOMMU, Pre-boot DMA protection) and `cat /sys/bus/thunderbolt/devices/domain0/iommu_dma_protection` (`1`).

Known fault: the Thunderbolt controller (JHL6240, NVM 23.00, the latest on LVFS) sometimes fails to come back from S3 (`pcieport 0000:03:00.0: not ready 65535ms after resume; giving up`, then `failed to send driver ready to ICM`); the port recovers on hotplug. If it keeps happening, switch `ThunderboltBIOSAssistMode` or use `s2idle` and compare.

CPU power: on a lap the firmware's lap sensor (`/sys/devices/platform/thinkpad_acpi/dytc_lapmode` = `1`) drops the sustained limit (PL1, `/sys/class/powercap/intel-rapl-mmio:0/constraint_0_power_limit_uw`) to 10 W, so all-core load runs near 1.9 GHz at about 65°C. That keeps the case cool on purpose and is left alone; on a desk the limit comes back. The MSR limit (`intel-rapl:0`, 51 W) is not the one that binds. Heat check: temperatures are in `/sys/class/hwmon/*/temp*_input`, the fan in `/proc/acpi/ibm/fan`, throttling in `/sys/devices/system/cpu/cpu0/thermal_throttle/` (the BIOS TCC offset makes it throttle at 97°C).

smartmontools and nvme-cli are diagnostics only: `sudo smartctl -a /dev/nvme0`, `sudo nvme smart-log /dev/nvme0`.
